    Before you go

    What to carry

    • Government photo ID for every adult (needed for forest permits)
    • Cap, sunglasses & sunscreen — there is little shade on the water
    • Reusable water bottle — plastic is banned inside the park
    • Binoculars and a camera with zoom
    • Light jacket in winter mornings
    • Earthy-coloured clothes; avoid bright colours

    Safari etiquette

    Respect the wild

    • Keep hands and feet inside the boat at all times
    • Stay quiet near wildlife — no music, no shouting
    • Never feed or tease animals
    • Wear the life jacket provided
    • Follow your boatman's and guide's instructions
    • Leave nothing behind but footprints

    Questions

    Boat safari FAQ

    How long is a boat safari?
    Usually 2.5 to 5 hours depending on the ghat and route. A trip from Chandbali to Khola alone takes about 2.5 hours on the Baitarani river.
    How many people fit in a boat?
    Boats typically carry 5 to 20 people depending on size. Tell us your group size and we'll arrange the right boat.
    Is the forest entry fee included?
    No. Forest entry, camera and guide fees are paid separately as per current Forest Department rates. We'll help you with the permits.
    When is the park closed?
    Every year from 1 May to 31 July for crocodile nesting, and for a few days in January for the crocodile and bird census. Dates are announced by the Forest Department — ask us before you plan.
    Will we definitely see crocodiles?
    It's wildlife, so nothing is guaranteed — but sightings are very common, especially on sunny winter mornings when crocodiles bask on the banks.
    How do I pay?
    We confirm availability on WhatsApp first, then share payment details. UPI and cash accepted.
