Getting here

How to reach

  1. By air: Biju Patnaik International Airport, Bhubaneswar. Chandbali is about 185 km (around 4 hours) by road via Bhadrak.
  2. By train: Bhadrak railway station is the nearest major station. Take a taxi or bus to Chandbali.
  3. By road: Reach Chandbali, then continue to Kasturikona Ghat. Call us when you reach Chandbali and we'll guide you in.
  4. By boat: From the ghat, boats go deep into Bhitarkanika — we'll book yours.

FAQ

Before you travel

Is there mobile network at the cottage?
Mobile coverage is available around Chandbali; signal can be weak inside the park. Tell your family you may be offline during the safari.
Do you arrange pickup?
Ask us on WhatsApp — we can guide you from Chandbali and help arrange local transport.
Is it safe for children and elders?
Yes. Boats come with life jackets and experienced boatmen. Our family cottages are close to the dining area.
When is the park closed?
1 May to 31 July every year, and a few days in January for the census.
