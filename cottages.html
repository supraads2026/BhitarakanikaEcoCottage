House notes

Check-in & check-out
We share check-in and check-out times when you book. Early arrivals for morning safaris can usually be managed — just tell us on WhatsApp.
Meals
Home-cooked Odia meals are served at the cottage. Tell us about any dietary needs in advance.
Plastic-free park
Plastic is banned inside Bhitarkanika National Park. Please carry a reusable water bottle — we'll refill it.
Payment
We confirm your booking and share payment details on WhatsApp. UPI and cash accepted.
