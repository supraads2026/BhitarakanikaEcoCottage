Our Story

A quiet home at the edge of the wild

On the riverbank at Kasturikona Ghat, near Chandbali, the tide comes and goes past our door — and just beyond lies one of India's largest mangrove forests.

We are a small, family-run stay. Simple, comfortable cottages, honest home food, and a team that knows the river. We arrange your boat safari, guide you through permits and timings, and leave you free to slow down, listen and look.

  • Boat safaris from 5 ghats
  • Help with forest permits
  • Fresh Odia meals & river fish
  • Family & group friendly

01 // THE RIVER

The lost world, at our doorstep

Bhitarkanika is India's second-largest mangrove ecosystem — a maze of tidal creeks where the Brahmani, Baitarani and Dhamra rivers meet the Bay of Bengal.

Discover the park →

02 // THE STAY

Cottages that touch the earth lightly

Simple, clean and close to nature — wake to birdsong, sip tea by the river, and come home to a warm Odia meal after the safari.

View the cottages →

03 // THE SAFARI

Kingdom of the saltwater crocodile

About 70% of India's estuarine crocodiles live here — 1,858 were counted in the 2026 census, including rare white ones. Watch them bask on the mudbanks from your boat.

Book a boat →

04 // THE WILD

Birds, turtles & tides

Thousands of herons nest at Bagagahana, winter brings migratory birds, and nearby Gahirmatha is one of the world's great Olive Ridley turtle nesting beaches.
