## 2. Bhitarkanika National Park — key facts for the site

- **What it is:** India's second-largest mangrove ecosystem, in Kendrapara district; a Ramsar wetland.
- **Saltwater crocodiles:** about 70% of India's estuarine crocodiles live here. The Jan 2026 census
  counted **1,858** crocodiles, including rare white/albino ones. 117 nests were recorded in 2026.
- **Birds:** Bagagahana heronry (near Surajpore creek) — thousands of nesting water birds;
  winter migrants Nov–Feb.
- **Turtles:** Gahirmatha beach is one of the world's largest Olive Ridley nesting sites
  (mass nesting typically Feb–Mar; e.g. 4.07 lakh turtles in March 2020). **VERIFY yearly.**
- **Visitors:** about 1 lakh tourists a year.

## 4. Boats & access

- Main gates: **Khola** and **Gupti**; **Dangmal** is the central tourist hub (crocodile centre).
- Motorboats (roughly 5–20 people) can be hired from Chandbali, Gupti, Khola and Dhamara.
- Chandbali → Khola: about 2.5 h by boat on the Baitarani river.
- Reference fares seen online (for context only, **not** our prices):
  ~₹3,000 per boat for a 2.5–3 h safari (up to 10 people) + guide ₹1,500–2,000;
  Govt listing ~₹4,000 per sortie Chandbali → Dangmal (18-seat boat).
